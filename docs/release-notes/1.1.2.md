## JottaFS 1.1.2

### Changed

- **Finder's own files stay on your Mac.** Finder writes hidden files into
  the folders that you use: `.DS_Store` files with the view settings of a
  folder, and `._` files that hold the Finder tags, comments and download
  information of a file. Before, JottaFS uploaded these files to Jottacloud,
  where they showed as extra files on jottacloud.com and on other devices.
  Now JottaFS keeps them on your Mac only. Your tags and view settings stay
  when you rename, move or put back files and folders, and when you unmount
  the disk. Other devices do not see them. Such files that are already on
  Jottacloud stay there, but JottaFS does not show them.
- **Time Machine does not offer Jottacloud disks.** Time Machine cannot back
  up to a Jottacloud disk, but its settings showed the disks in the list of
  backup disks. Now Time Machine does not show them.
- **No file-event log on Jottacloud disks.** macOS added a `.fseventsd`
  folder with log files to each Jottacloud disk, and JottaFS uploaded them.
  Now macOS does not keep this log on Jottacloud disks. A `.fseventsd` folder
  that is already on Jottacloud is not shown.

### Known limits

- JottaFS keeps a full copy of a file on your Mac before the upload starts,
  because Jottacloud must know the checksum of the complete file first. Your
  Mac must have free space for the largest file that you copy. Files upload
  one at a time.
- Finder does not show changes that you make on jottacloud.com or on a
  different device until it reads the folder again. Close and open the
  folder, or unmount the disk and mount it again.
- You cannot rename the disk in Finder. Use **Advanced** in JottaFS.

### Requirements

- A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 27 or later.
- A Jottacloud account.

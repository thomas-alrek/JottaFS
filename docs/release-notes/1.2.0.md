## JottaFS 1.2.0

### New

- **A new look with Liquid Glass.** JottaFS uses the Liquid Glass design of
  macOS 27. The window is translucent, so your desktop shows through it,
  blurred. The buttons are made of glass, and Unmount and Clear Cache are
  red because they remove something.
- **A new app icon.** The icon has layers that react to light, like the
  icons of the apps that come with macOS. It looks the same in light and
  dark mode, and it follows your choice when you select tinted or clear
  icons in System Settings.
- **Settings is at the top of the window.** Click the gear button in the
  upper-right corner, or choose **JottaFS → Settings**.
- **Clearer Settings in light mode.** The sections in the Settings window
  are easier to see.

### Fixed

- The icon of the selected location no longer disappears in the highlight.

### Known limits

- Names with the characters `" * : < > ? | % \` cannot be used, because
  Jottacloud cannot store them. Finder then shows "an unknown error
  occurred (error code -50)". Images that you save from websites often
  have a `?` in the name. Rename the file, then copy it again.
- JottaFS keeps a full copy of a file on your Mac before the upload starts,
  because Jottacloud must know the checksum of the complete file first. Your
  Mac must have free space for the largest file that you copy. Files upload
  one at a time.
- Finder does not show changes that you make on jottacloud.com or on a
  different device until it reads the folder again. Close and open the
  folder, or unmount the disk and mount it again.
- You cannot rename the disk in Finder. Use **Advanced** in JottaFS.

### Requirements

- A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 27 or later.
- A Jottacloud account.

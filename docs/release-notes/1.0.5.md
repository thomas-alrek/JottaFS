## JottaFS 1.0.5

### Fixed

- **Mount at login works after a restart.** When you log in, JottaFS now
  waits for the network connection (for example, for Wi-Fi to connect)
  before it mounts your locations. If a mount fails, JottaFS tries again for
  up to 3 minutes.
- **No window at login.** When JottaFS mounts your locations at login, no
  window opens and no icon shows in the Dock. The window opens only if a
  mount still fails.
- **JottaFS does not open again by itself after a restart.** Before, macOS
  could open JottaFS again at login if it was open when you shut down, and
  the window showed.
- **No error before the network is ready.** If you open JottaFS before your
  Mac is connected, the list of locations shows "Waiting for a network
  connection…" and loads when the connection is ready.
- **The JottaFS window opens in front.** When you open JottaFS, its window
  is the active window.

### Changed

- Mount at login now uses a background item. Your current settings for mount
  at login stay the same. If macOS asks, allow **JottaFS** in **System
  Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions**.

### Known limits

- Finder does not show changes that you make on jottacloud.com or on a
  different device until it reads the folder again. Close and open the
  folder, or unmount the disk and mount it again.
- Your Mac must have free space for each file while it uploads.
- You cannot rename the disk in Finder. Use **Advanced** in JottaFS.

### Requirements

- A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 27 or later.
- A Jottacloud account.

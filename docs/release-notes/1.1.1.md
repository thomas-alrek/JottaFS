## JottaFS 1.1.1

### Fixed

- **A file that is too large for your Mac stops at once.** JottaFS keeps a
  copy of each file on your Mac until the upload is complete. Before, when
  you copied a file that was larger than the free space on your Mac, the
  copy continued until your disk was full and then failed. Now, when you
  copy with Finder or `cp`, the copy stops at once with the error "not
  enough free space", before JottaFS writes the file.
- **Uploads get space from the download cache.** When your Mac does not
  have sufficient free space for a file that you copy to a Jottacloud disk,
  JottaFS first deletes the oldest data from its download cache.
- **The DMG window shows its background again.** The first DMG of JottaFS
  1.1.0 opened without its background and icon layout. The DMG of 1.1.0 was
  replaced, and the release process now makes sure that this cannot occur
  again.

### Known limits

- JottaFS keeps a full copy of a file on your Mac before the upload starts,
  because Jottacloud must know the checksum of the complete file first. Your
  Mac must have free space for the largest file that you copy. Files upload
  one at a time.
- Finder does not show changes that you make on jottacloud.com or on a
  different device until it reads the folder again. Close and open the
  folder, or unmount the disk and mount it again.
- You cannot rename the disk in Finder. Use **Advanced** in JottaFS.

### Requirements

- A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 27 or later.
- A Jottacloud account.

## JottaFS 1.0.0

The first release of JottaFS. Use your Jottacloud files in Finder on your
Mac, as a disk.

JottaFS is not an official Jottacloud product. Jottacloud AS does not make,
support or approve it.

### Features

- **Sign in with your browser**, or with a personal login token. JottaFS
  does not see your password.
- **Mount one Jottacloud location as a disk**: Archive, Sync, Shared or a
  folder from a computer backup. You can give the disk a different name in
  **Advanced**.
- **Read and write files.** Open, copy, change, rename, move and delete
  files and folders.
- **Upload progress in Finder.** A Finder copy is complete only when the
  upload is complete. If the connection stops for a short time, JottaFS
  tries the upload again.
- **Finder's Trash works with the Jottacloud trash**: Move to Trash, Put Back
  and Empty Trash.
- **Mount at login**, without a window.
- **Automatic updates.** JottaFS tells you when a new version is available
  and installs it for you.

### Requirements

- A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 27 or later.
- A Jottacloud account.

### Install

1. Download `JottaFS-1.0.0.dmg` below and open it.
2. Drag **JottaFS** to **Applications**.
3. Open JottaFS from Applications.
4. When JottaFS asks, turn on **JottaFS** in **System Settings → General →
   Login Items & Extensions → File System Extensions**.
5. Sign in, select a location and click **Mount**.

### Important

- **Empty Trash deletes permanently.** The Trash of a mounted disk shows the
  Jottacloud trash for that location, also items that you deleted on
  jottacloud.com or on a different device. When you empty the Trash, these
  items are deleted permanently.
- **`rm` in Terminal deletes permanently**, as on a local disk.

### Known limits

- You can mount one location at a time.
- You cannot rename the disk in Finder. Use **Advanced** in JottaFS.
- You cannot open files that are in the Trash.
- The maximum file size for a write is 2 GiB.
- Jottacloud sends downloads at approximately 5–6 MB/s.

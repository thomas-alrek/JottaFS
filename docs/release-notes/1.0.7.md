## JottaFS 1.0.7

### New

- **Sidebar icon.** Jottacloud disks now show their own icon in the Finder
  sidebar: a drive with the Jottacloud logo, in the same style as Macintosh
  HD. Before, the sidebar showed the JottaFS app icon.

  After you update, Finder can show the old icon until you relaunch it.
  Hold Option, Control-click the Finder icon in the Dock and choose
  **Relaunch**.

### Known limits

- Finder does not show changes that you make on jottacloud.com or on a
  different device until it reads the folder again. Close and open the
  folder, or unmount the disk and mount it again.
- Your Mac must have free space for each file while it uploads.
- You cannot rename the disk in Finder. Use **Advanced** in JottaFS.

### Requirements

- A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 27 or later.
- A Jottacloud account.

## JottaFS 1.1.0

### New

- **Norwegian.** JottaFS is now available in Norwegian (Bokmål). JottaFS
  uses Norwegian when Norwegian is in your preferred languages in **System
  Settings → General → Language & Region**. Otherwise it uses English. In
  Norwegian, Archive, Sync, Shared and Photos show as Arkiv, Synk, Delt and
  Bilder, also as the names of the disks.
- **Your sign-in stays active.** macOS opens JottaFS without a window two
  times a day for a moment, to keep your Jottacloud sign-in active when you
  do not use your disks. You can see this item in **System Settings →
  General → Login Items & Extensions**.
- **Clear message when your sign-in ends.** If you remove JottaFS on
  jottacloud.com, or if Jottacloud ends the sign-in, JottaFS unmounts your
  disks and asks you to sign in again. Before, the disks stayed in Finder
  but did not work.
- **JottaFS in your list of devices.** On jottacloud.com, **Settings →
  Security** now shows JottaFS with the name of your Mac. Before, it showed
  "Jottacloud CLI".
- **Settings button.** Click the gear button in the lower-left corner of the
  JottaFS window to open the settings.
- **Extensions in Settings.** **JottaFS → Settings** now has switches for the
  file system extension and the Finder extension. A switch opens the correct
  page in System Settings.

### Faster

- **Faster downloads of large files.** JottaFS now downloads more parts of a
  file at the same time when you read a large file from start to end, for
  example when you copy it to your Mac. Small files use fewer connections.

### Changed

- **Sign out also signs out on Jottacloud.** When you sign out in JottaFS,
  JottaFS now also removes the sign-in from your Jottacloud account.
- **Sidebar icon.** Jottacloud disks show the standard drive icon in the
  Finder sidebar, the same as Macintosh HD. After you update, relaunch
  Finder to see the new icon: hold Option, Control-click the Finder icon in
  the Dock and choose **Relaunch**.

### Fixed

- **JottaFS sees when you turn off its extension.** If you turn off JottaFS
  in **File System Extensions**, the JottaFS window now shows how to turn it
  on again.
- **The Settings window has no scroll bar.**

### Known limits

- JottaFS keeps a full copy of a file on your Mac before the upload starts,
  because Jottacloud must know the checksum of the complete file first. Your
  Mac must have free space for the file that is uploading. Files upload one
  at a time.
- Finder does not show changes that you make on jottacloud.com or on a
  different device until it reads the folder again. Close and open the
  folder, or unmount the disk and mount it again.
- You cannot rename the disk in Finder. Use **Advanced** in JottaFS.

### Requirements

- A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 27 or later.
- A Jottacloud account.
